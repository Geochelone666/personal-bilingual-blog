Besides programming, I also invest in stocks. Recently, there’s been a flurry of major events: the midterm elections, the Fed’s interest rate hike, and Ant Group’s IPO. Personally, I’m interested in technology and have a preference for tech companies—and I understand them better than most. But not everyone is a fan of technological progress.Technological development is cool in and of itself, but it’s the resulting disruption to various industries that has everyone worried. It seems that Trump’s way of “Making America Great Again” is to prioritize business leadership, but the Democrats clearly don’t see it that way.The Democrats scored a landslide victory in the midterm elections. Combined with the Federal Reserve’s new framework—market self-regulation, no bailouts, and no guidance—this has made the current tech revolution even more chaotic than before. Ant Group’s current situation clearly doesn’t support the valuation they’re seeking. Could they still have a secret weapon up their sleeve? (But that would also hit a nerve with the Democrats.)With so many factors converging, it doesn’t look like tech stocks will have an easy time of it. This convergence of factors could very well trigger a panic sell-off, but will this mark the bursting of the AI bubble? I don’t think the time has come yet; the crash that signals the true bursting of the AI bubble will certainly be far more devastating than this one.
