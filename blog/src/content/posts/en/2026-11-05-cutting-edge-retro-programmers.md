I’m a computer science student—so why am I starting a blog? Because I want to dabble in some retro stuff. Although blogging used to be all the rage, with the advancement of technology, blogs have become a sort of “archaeological” website. I always enjoy browsing through blogs created by others—it’s like digging for treasure. It’s fascinating to see how the innovative blogs created by young people back in the day have gradually become outdated.Blog moderators also enjoyed their professional heyday as the times changed, only to be suddenly upended by AI. AI, as a technological revolution poised to replace programmers, is much like how textile machines replaced seamstresses and trains replaced coachmen.I always thought industrialization and increased efficiency were good things. But now I’m finally realizing the joy of handcrafted creations. Although in that era, most textile workers did the job to make money, there were certainly some who genuinely enjoyed the process of weaving. Industrialization brings more widespread products to society (such as mass-produced standardized clothing, public trains, and increasingly simplified programming).But there are still people who find joy in this process, even though these days, weaving or horseback riding no longer brings any financial reward. It still exists as a hobby. I just feel sorry for those who rush into studying computer science solely for the high pay. All in all, I also enjoy the fun of programming—so let me just be a cutting-edge retro guy :)
